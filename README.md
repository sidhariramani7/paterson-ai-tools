# Paterson AI Tools

Three production marketing tools built using Claude Code for Paterson Capital. No dev team. All live and actively used.

---

## 1. IPO Dashboard

Live IPO tracker showing open, upcoming, and recently closed NSE mainboard and SME IPOs — with subscription figures, price bands, open/close dates, and allotment status. Auto-updates daily.

**Why it matters:** IPO-related searches are Paterson's highest-intent traffic. The dashboard captures that intent at the moment people are actively researching, and converts it into enquiries.

## 2. AI Research Blog Pipeline

Takes Paterson's daily research and analyst reports, automatically converts them into SEO-first, brand-consistent articles, publishes them to the website, and distributes a formatted copy to the sales and RM team every morning — with zero manual effort after setup.

**Why it matters:** Paterson's analysts publish research daily. The old process: it sat in PDFs, unseen. The new process: every piece of research becomes a published article and a morning sales touchpoint automatically.

**Stats:**
- 1,000+ articles published to date
- ~5 articles per day
- Zero manual effort
